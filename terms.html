The plain-language version

If it is not in the record, it is not a claim.

TampalIdea keeps a shared operating record for a project’s people, proposed ownership and decisions. Participation, discussion, a chat message or work performed does not by itself create an ownership allocation. The record is the source used to establish what was claimed and when.

01

When the clock starts

The claim window starts at the project’s recorded creation timestamp in TampalIdea. It ends 14 calendar days later at the same time. The creation timestamp and the audit trail are the controlling record for this purpose.

02

What counts as a claim

A claim is valid only when an audit entry records the claimant, the project, the ownership share or role being claimed, and the basis or source reference. It must be entered through an authorised TampalIdea or Orin workflow.

03

What does not count

Unrecorded conversations, suggestions, introductions, drafts, effort or access to a group do not constitute a recorded ownership claim. They may support a later discussion, but they do not change the ledger unless an authorised audit entry is made.

04

After 14 days

At the close of the claim window, any ownership not validly claimed in the audit trail defaults to Sayyid Khan. This does not disturb a share already recorded for another contributor. A late claim has no effect unless Sayyid Khan approves and the revised outcome is recorded in the audit trail.

05

How to make a claim

State the project, your claimed role or percentage, and the basis for it through the project’s linked Orin channel or another founder-authorised ledger workflow. Check that the resulting audit entry accurately reflects the claim before the window closes.

06

Disputes and corrections

Raise a factual error before the deadline through the project’s authorised channel. Corrections, approvals and any changed allocation must be captured as a new audit entry; the audit trail remains append-only and shows the history of the decision.

How a decision is read

The audit trail decides the record.

  1. CreatedThe project timestamp opens the 14-day claim window.
  2. ClaimedAn authorised audit entry identifies the claimant and their proposed allocation.
  3. ResolvedAt the deadline, unrecorded ownership defaults to Sayyid Khan; any exception must be recorded.

General terms

Use of TampalIdea

TampalIdea is a project IP ledger and operating record. Contributors are responsible for ensuring that details they ask to be recorded are accurate and that they have the authority to provide them. Project records may be scoped to a linked WhatsApp group; access to a group record does not grant ownership or permission to disclose it elsewhere.

These terms set the operating default for the TampalIdea record. They do not replace a signed contract, employment terms, statutory rights, or independent legal advice. Where a signed written agreement or a founder-authorised audit entry expressly sets a different arrangement for a project, that documented arrangement takes priority to the extent it applies.

Sayyid Khan may update this policy for future project records. A change does not rewrite an existing audit trail; a different outcome for an existing project must be expressly recorded in that project’s audit trail.
